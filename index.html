ABOUT VERTICAL FARMING

What Is Vertical Farming?

Vertical farming is a method of growing crops in vertically stacked layers or specially designed structures. It can be used in indoor spaces, greenhouses and urban environments. Depending on the system, it may use soil, water-based nutrients or mist to support plant growth.
